## Starting point

Critical services depended on integrations that had accumulated over many years. Documentation was incomplete, responsibilities were distributed across teams and suppliers, and parts of the current architecture were understood mainly through operational experience.

The organisation needed to modernise the landscape without interrupting services or carrying poorly understood dependencies into the target architecture.

## The decision to make

The central question was not simply which technology should replace the legacy components. The organisation first needed to understand which integrations were business-critical, how information moved between systems, where ownership was unclear and which parts could be renewed safely as coherent transition slices.

## How we worked

- Consolidated the available architecture material, system inventories and interface documentation into a common discovery view.
- Used AI-assisted analysis to identify connections, repeated patterns, contradictions and gaps that required further investigation.
- Validated the findings with architects, service owners and people who held practical knowledge of the operating environment.
- Mapped integration dependencies, information flows, responsibilities and areas of operational risk.
- Evaluated modernisation opportunities against business criticality, architectural direction, feasibility and interdependence.
- Structured the findings into near-term risk-reduction actions, target-architecture decisions and a sequenced renewal roadmap.

AI accelerated the analysis of a large and uneven body of material. It did not replace architectural judgement or validation by the people responsible for the services.

## What the client gained

The engagement produced an evidence-based view of the current integration landscape, a visible list of unresolved questions and ownership decisions, and a practical roadmap for modernising APIs, integrations and shared platform capabilities in manageable stages.

This gave the organisation a more defensible basis for deciding what to retain, replace, decouple or investigate further before implementation.
